The Fastcloud Windows installer includes quantized CLAP model weights from
Xenova/larger_clap_music_and_speech, derived from
laion/larger_clap_music_and_speech. The original LAION model is licensed under
the Apache License, Version 2.0. The bundled weights are obtained from the
pinned Xenova revision documented in tools/prepare_private_clap.ps1.

Model: https://huggingface.co/laion/larger_clap_music_and_speech
Quantized weights: https://huggingface.co/Xenova/larger_clap_music_and_speech
